S-35/53LC20D is a pair of SFP transceivers, the S-35LC20D is a 1.25G Single Mode optical SFP module with an LC connector, T1310nm/R1550nm, which works for connections up to 20km and supports DDM. The S-53LC20D is a 1.25G Single Mode optical SFP transceiver with an LC connector, T1550nm/R1310nm, which works for connections up to 20km and supports DDM. See the S-3553LC20D product page for full specifications and brochures.
